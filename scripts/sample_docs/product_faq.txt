Docklight Demo Co. — Product FAQ (Sample Fake Data)

Q: What is the refund window for a purchased subscription?
A: Subscriptions can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase if fewer
than 100 API calls have been made on the account.

Q: Does the product support single sign-on (SSO)?
A: Yes. SSO via SAML 2.0 is available on the Team and Enterprise plans.
Google and Microsoft social login are available on all plans.

Q: What happens to my data if I cancel my plan?
A: Your data is retained for 30 days after cancellation in case you resubscribe,
then permanently deleted. You can request immediate deletion by contacting support.

Q: Is there a free tier?
A: Yes, the free tier includes up to 3 documents and 100 questions per month,
intended for evaluating the product before upgrading.
